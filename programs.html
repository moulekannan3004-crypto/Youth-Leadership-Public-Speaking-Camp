Frequently Asked Questions

Everything parents need to know about safety, peer grouping, and what to expect on Day 1.

Over 70% of our campers enter on Day 1 describing themselves as "very shy" or "terrified" of school presentations. We never force children onto a high-stakes stage. Our proven cognitive desensitization starts with 1-on-1 mentor games, seated circle sharing, and playful puppet storytelling before ever introducing a podium. By Week 2, children who initially wouldn't make eye contact eagerly volunteer for the microphone.

On Day 1, our coaching staff conducts an informal, low-pressure diagnostic assessment. Campers are matched into small squads of 6 students who share similar maturity levels and complementary communication temperaments, ensuring every child feels safe, heard, and appropriately challenged.

All VoxLeaders mentors undergo rigorous FBI-level background checks, child-welfare certifications, and CPR/First-Aid training. We maintain an ironclad 1:6 staff-to-student supervision ratio at all times, with full-time registered nurses on campus and secure, badge-only facility access.
